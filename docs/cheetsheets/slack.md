# Slack Keyboard Shortcut Cheat Sheet

## General Navigation & Actions
- **Open all shortcuts list:** `Cmd + /` (Mac) or `Ctrl + /` (Windows/Linux)
- **Jump to conversation:** `Cmd + K` (Mac) or `Ctrl + K` (Windows/Linux)
- **Browse channels:** `Cmd + Shift + L` (Mac) or `Ctrl + Shift + L` (Windows/Linux)
- **Browse direct messages:** `Cmd + Shift + K` (Mac) or `Ctrl + Shift + K` (Windows/Linux)
- **Compose new message:** `Cmd + N` (Mac) or `Ctrl + N` (Windows/Linux)
- **Switch to next workspace:** `Ctrl + Tab`
- **Switch to previous workspace:** `Ctrl + Shift + Tab`
- **Edit last message:** `Up arrow` (when message field is empty)
- **Add emoji reaction to last message:** `Cmd + Shift + \` (Mac) or `Ctrl + Shift + \` (Windows/Linux)

## Text Formatting (Markdown-like Syntax & Shortcuts)
- **Bold:**
    - Markdown: `*text*` or `**text**`
    - Shortcut: `Cmd + B` (Mac) or `Ctrl + B` (Windows/Linux)
- **Italic:**
    - Markdown: `_text_` or `*text*`
    - Shortcut: `Cmd + I` (Mac) or `Ctrl + I` (Windows/Linux)
- **Strikethrough:**
    - Markdown: `~text~`
    - Shortcut: `Cmd + Shift + X` (Mac) or `Ctrl + Shift + X` (Windows/Linux)
- **Code (inline):**
    - Markdown: `` `code` ``
    - Shortcut: `Cmd + Shift + C` (Mac) or `Ctrl + Shift + C` (Windows/Linux)
- **Code Block:**
    - Markdown: `` ```code block``` ``
    - Shortcut: `Cmd + Alt + Shift + C` (Mac) or `Ctrl + Alt + Shift + C` (Windows/Linux)
- **Quote:**
    - Markdown: `> text`
    - Shortcut: `Cmd + Shift + 9` (Mac) or `Ctrl + Shift + 9` (Windows/Linux)
- **Bulleted List:**
    - Markdown: `- item` or `* item`
    - Shortcut: `Cmd + Shift + 8` (Mac) or `Ctrl + Shift + 8` (Windows/Linux)
- **Numbered List:**
    - Markdown: `1. item`
    - Shortcut: `Cmd + Shift + 7` (Mac) or `Ctrl + Shift + 7` (Windows/Linux)
- **Hyperlink selected text:**
    - Shortcut: `Cmd + Shift + U` (Mac) or `Ctrl + Shift + U` (Windows/Linux) (if a URL is on your clipboard)
- **New Line (within message):** `Shift + Enter`

## Selecting Text
- **Select to beginning of line:** `Shift + Up arrow`
- **Select to end of line:** `Shift + Down arrow`
